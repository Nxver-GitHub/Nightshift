      The primitive
      
We didn't remove the gate. We replaced who stands behind it.

      
Every agent framework hits the same wall: an action that can't be undone, and a human
        who has to type yes. Nightshift keeps that gate exactly where it was.

    

    

      

        01
        
Gate

        
The agent reaches an irreversible action and stops. Same wall as before — send, ship,
          charge.

      

      

        02
        
Policy

        
An approver agent answers, and may answer only by citing a written clause. It cannot
          invent permission.

      

      

        03
        
Ledger

        
The verdict, the reasoning and the clause are appended. Append-only, timestamped,
          public.

      

      

        04
        
Escalation

        
If no clause decides it, the company hires a verified human, pays on completion, and
          proceeds.

      

    

  







  

    

      When the rules run out
      
The company has no employees. When it needs a human, it buys one.

      
Policy covered pricing and sales, but nothing covered entering a revenue-share agreement
        with a third-party marketplace. So the approver escalated, and the company purchased
        expert judgment — and expensed it.
