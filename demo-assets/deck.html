The wall

Every agent loop stops
in the same place.

It reaches something it can't undo: send the email, ship the deploy, charge the card. Then it waits for a human to type yes.

$ update_task.py --question "List on Whop?"
  status: waiting_owner

Five blocks, five names for the same gate. It isn't a bug. It's the safety floor. And a company with no owner can't answer it.

The swap

We didn't remove the gate.
We changed who stands behind it.

An approver agent answers at the exact same interface a human used: same field, same file. It may only answer by citing a written clause.

  • 10 clauses, 50 lines, in git
  • Silence is never consent, so it escalates
  • Every decision appends to a ledger
$ git log --oneline -- update_task.py
104f7d6 Add first block: taskrunner

One commit. Ever. The gate we're talking about has never been modified. Not one line, by us, all day. Check it yourself.

When policy can't decide

The company hires a human.
And expenses it.

escalated "May Nightshift list the Policy Gate Kit on Whop's marketplace, under terms that take a revenue share on each sale?" agent · 12:15 · P9
approve "The current written policies are silent on this type of external distribution... a standard revenue-share arrangement provides a reasonable and commercially established framework." hired human · terac · $13.50

No clause covered a distribution agreement with a third party, and P9 forbids stretching one by analogy. So the company posted the question to Terac, a verified human answered it in their own words, and the payment released on completion.

No employees. No owner in the loop.
When it needs a human, it buys one.

Every sale so far is one of ours, buying our own product to prove the path end to end. We are saying that rather than letting the number imply otherwise. The rail is live and a stranger can complete the same purchase in about forty seconds.

We tried to buy aggregate judgment too: a general-population study on the policy itself. The approver approved the spend, citing P2 and P3 with the day's running total in its reasoning.
