# Positioning & offer — Acme Flows

> ⚠️ Fictional example, in the founder's voice for illustration.

## What we do (one line)
We run the back-office of your dental practice so your front desk can focus on patients.

## The problem we solve
Independent practices lose real money to no-shows and drown in insurance paperwork, but they're too
small to hire an operations person. The work is repetitive and rules-based — perfect for agents,
painful for humans.

## Ideal customer (ICP)
Independent dental practices, 1–3 chairs, in Belgium, owner-operated, no dedicated office manager.
Not chains (they have staff), not solo hygienists (too little volume).

## The offer
Monthly retainer, €600–900/practice. We set up and run three flows: no-show recovery, appointment
follow-ups, and insurance paperwork prep. Done-for-you: the dentist approves, we operate. Out of
scope: clinical anything, and replacing their booking software — we sit on top of it.

## Why us / the wedge
Founder was an office manager — we speak the buyer's language and know the exact workflows. And
because agents do the work, we can price below what an employee costs while covering more hours.

## Anti-position (what we refuse to be)
Not a software subscription the dentist has to learn and operate. If it becomes another dashboard
they have to check, we've failed.

## Assumptions to test
- Riskiest belief: practices will trust an outside service to touch patient communication. Test with
  the first two before scaling.
