## The company in a nutshell
Acme Flows builds and runs the back-office automation for **independent dental practices** — booking
follow-ups, insurance paperwork prep, and no-show recovery — as a done-for-you service, not a tool
the dentist has to operate. One founder, agent-run from day one.

## The founder(s)
Robin Vega — founder, solo. Wants to keep doing sales calls and practice relationships personally;
wants everything else (inbox, bookkeeping prep, outreach drafting) handled by agents. Detail:
`acme/notes/people.md`.

## Language & style
- Respond in English. Direct and concrete; Robin dislikes fluff and wants sources on any claim.

## How this brain is organized
- `acme/` — the company: identity, offer, positioning, model, its projects.
- `clients/` — one folder per dental practice, created as they sign.
- `misc/` — anything without an owner yet.
